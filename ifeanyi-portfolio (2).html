About me

I'm Ifeanyi, a full-time HTML and CSS developer and web designer who fell in love with computers before I ever wrote a line of code. As a boy I was the one neighbours called when their computer froze. I didn't know what I was doing at first. I just refused to leave until it worked.

That stubbornness became my career. In 2023 I started programming properly, teaching myself web development and design in the evenings, and built my first real website for a small shop owner who paid me in a good meal and a lot of encouragement. Since then I've worked on payments, marketplaces and learning tools, always for people who need software to be simple and dependable. I'm now a 100-level student at Edo State University, and in 2025 I joined CCT Programming School to deepen my skills.

What drives me is the moment a tool I built makes someone's day easier. Outside of work I help other beginners get started with code, contribute to open source and join community coding meetups.
